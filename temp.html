Boudhanath Stupa

4.5 (128 reviews)
Temple

About

Boudhanath is one of the largest stupas in the world and the holiest Tibetan Buddhist temple outside Tibet. It is the center of Tibetan culture in Kathmandu and rich in Buddhist symbolism. The stupa is located in the town of Boudha, on the eastern outskirts of Kathmandu.

Highlights

  • One of the largest spherical stupas in Nepal
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Vibrant Tibetan community surrounding the stupa
  • Best visited in the morning or evening for prayer ceremonies

Best Time to Visit

Early morning or late afternoon when the prayer wheels are spinning and monks are chanting.
