Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hidden Nepal?

Hidden Nepal is a community-driven platform for discovering and sharing lesser-known places, experiences, and stories from Nepal. We connect travelers, locals, and contributors to help everyone explore Nepal beyond the usual tourist spots.

How can I contribute to Hidden Nepal?

Anyone can contribute by sharing their favorite hidden places, uploading photos, writing reviews, or providing travel tips. Simply register, log in, and use the contributor dashboard to add new content.

Is Hidden Nepal free to use?

Yes, Hidden Nepal is free for all users. You can browse places, read reviews, and contribute without any charges.

How do I find local guides or authentic experiences?

Browse our listings and reviews to find local guides, unique experiences, and tips from real travelers and contributors. We encourage responsible and sustainable travel.

How is content verified?

Our team and community moderators review submissions for accuracy and authenticity. We rely on user feedback and reporting to maintain quality.

Who can I contact for support?

For any questions or support, use our contact page or email us at support@hiddennepal.com.
